How to Play

  • Controls (1P): Arrow Keys to move.
  • Controls (2P):G P1 uses WASD, P2 uses Arrow Keys.
  • Goal: Varies by mode! Survive, get a high score, or trap your opponent.
  • Moving Blocks: Avoid these! They move on their own (except in Tron).
  • Red Food: +1 point (No food in Tron).
  • Gold Food: +5 points, slows game.
  • Blue Food: +1 point, 5s invincibility.
  • Cyan Power-up: 2x Score for 10s.
  • Orange Power-up: 1-hit shield.
  • Green Power-up: Shrinks snake by 3.
  • Pink Power-up: Reverses controls for 7s.
  • Dark Curse: Blinds you for 7s!
