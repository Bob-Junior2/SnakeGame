How to Play

GOAL

Win the match based on the selected Game Mode (Health, Classic, Timed, or Sudden Death). Use power-ups to gain an advantage!

CONTROLS

Player 1 (Left Paddle):
Move: ARROW UP / ARROW DOWN
Dash: RIGHT SHIFT (or LEFT SHIFT in 1P)
Fire Laser: RIGHT CONTROL

Player 2 (Right Paddle / 2P Mode):
Move: W / S
Dash: LEFT SHIFT
Fire Laser: SPACEBAR

Game:
Pause / Resume: ESCAPE

ADVANCED MOVES

  • Spin: Hit the ball while your paddle is moving to add a curve.
  • Dash: A quick burst of speed on a 2-second cooldown.

POWER-UPS (Hit ball into them)

  • (M) Multiball: Spawns an extra ball.
  • (F) Fireball: Ball is worth 3 damage / points.
  • (S) Shrink: Makes opponent's paddle tiny.
  • (Z) Freeze: Freezes opponent for 2 seconds.
  • (?) Flip: Reverses opponent's controls.
  • (+) Shield: Blocks the next goal.
  • (L) Laser: Grants one laser shot.
